# 070 · Discussions on organizations: platform-moderated, with one official reply

**Date:** 2026-11-27
**Status:** Accepted, built the same day
**Context:** The user wants people to talk about organizations on their public pages: reviews, criticism, praise, questions. It should be public and use people's accounts, like the discussion on dictionary words ([#67](067-word-discussions-public-flat-comments-with-up-votes.md)), which was written for any target. A group is a different subject from a word, though. The group can answer back, has reasons to remove criticism, and may be sensitive or not know its page exists.
**Decision:**

- **The same discussion as on words** (public reading; commenting, up voting and reporting need a finished account; flat list; Top and Newest; plain text; delete your own; 5 comments an hour across all discussions), on `/organization/{handle}`, as a new target kind `organization`.
- **Comments only, no ratings.** No stars, scores or "recommend" counts. Up votes on comments show what people agree with. Small volunteer groups aren't reduced to a number that one grudge or a brigade can sink.
- **Super users moderate, through reports, on claimed organizations too.** Anyone signed in, including the group's owners and moderators, can report a comment. Only super users hide or delete, and every action goes in `adminLog` as on words. The group can't quietly remove criticism of itself. This loosens AGENTS.md's "super users stay out of claimed organizations" for **public comments only**. That rule protects members-only data, and these comments are public; super users still get no access to anything members-only.
- **One official reply per comment.** Owners and moderators can attach one reply to any comment, shown under it as from the organization ("Reply from South Bay Mutual Aid"), not from the person who wrote it. The person is recorded and appears in the organization's Recent actions. The list stays flat: nobody else replies. An official reply can be reported, and super users can hide or delete it like a comment.
- **Listed organizations only, claimed or unclaimed.** Never on unlisted ones, which exist for groups that don't want to be found (a tenant union organizing against its landlord). Unclaimed pages get it because comments there are useful ("they stopped meeting", "they helped me"), and super users, who act as their owners, can post the official reply until the group claims the page. On an unclaimed page it reads **"Reply from the Communalists team"**, never the organization's name: the group doesn't run the page, and a reply in its name would speak for it. Replies written before a claim keep that label afterward.

Defaults chosen with the design (change them if they don't fit):

- **All languages first.** Organizations aren't translated, so a reader's UI language says little about which comments matter to them. Each comment still has its language tag, for the screen reader voice and the marker.
- **No member or role badges on comments.** A member list can be private ([features/16](../features/16-organizations.md)), and a badge would reveal membership the group chose to hide. The official reply is the organization's voice.
- **The discussion follows the page.** Switching to unlisted or deleting (archiving) the organization hides its discussion without deleting it; listing it again or restoring brings it back. Claiming an unclaimed page keeps the discussion.

**Alternatives:** owners and moderators moderating their own page (simple, but they can delete honest criticism), or both with super users reversing (more moving parts, same pressure); star ratings or a yes/no recommend (easy to game, harsh on small groups); no replies (the group can only post an ordinary comment that sinks in the list); threaded replies (harder with a screen reader and to moderate); an owner switch to turn the discussion off (protects harassed groups but also hides criticism; reports and super users handle harassment instead); leaving out unclaimed pages (nobody criticized before they can answer, but loses useful signal, and super users can answer meanwhile); every organization, unlisted included (exposes groups that asked not to be found).
**Consequences:** Placed after the member list and before nearby groups (the user's choice). A new target kind, `canModerate` for organizations returning super users only, an official reply on the comment document (its text, language, date and uid kept server-side), and the target's visibility checked on every read and write. AGENTS.md's super-user rule names this exception. Reports and moderation now say which part (`comment` or `reply`); new `adminLog` actions `hideReply`, `unhideReply`, `deleteReply`, `replyComment`, and organization log actions `replyComment`, `deleteReply`.
